What happens with what you send

Atlas is a set of notes on how TPG works, kept up to date by an AI agent that reads TPG’s docs, Slack and meetings. Your tasks and the questions behind them become the test Atlas has to pass.

  1. Your questionsEach question you send, with the answer you know, becomes a test case.
  2. Asked twiceClaude with TPG’s docsClaude with Atlas
  3. ScoredEach reply is checked for the facts in your answer.
  4. Atlas improvesWhere it misses, we fix what it reads. Every update has to keep passing before it goes live.

So your questions are how we judge whether Atlas is any good. A question nothing could answer for you is the most useful one to send.
