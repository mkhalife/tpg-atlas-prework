Teach Atlas how TPG works

Atlas is an AI brain for how TPG works, built by the product team. Ten minutes of your real work is how we’ll know if it’s any good.

  • About 10 minutes
  • You approve every row
  • Anonymous

What Atlas is

  • An AI that knows TPG: strategy, OKRs, metric definitions, research, what’s been built
  • Kept up to date from docs, Slack and meetings
  • Plugged into the AI tools you already use

What you’ll do

  1. Open your AI with one click
  2. It drafts a few recent TPG tasks from your work with it
  3. Answer three quick questions
  4. Review the rows and click Send

What happens next

  • Each question you send becomes a test case
  • Atlas has to answer it correctly to pass
  • Every update has to keep passing

Questions

Is it anonymous?

Yes. No name or email is sent. The send page asks you to sign in with your Red Ventures Google account to keep it internal, and doesn’t record who you are.

What exactly gets sent?

Only the rows you approve: a few tasks, the questions about TPG each one needed, the answers if you know them, and where those answers live. Never a transcript or a quote from your chats.

Google asked me to sign in with an administrator account.

Pick your Red Ventures account anyway. If the page still doesn’t load, open the link again.

What if a task is sensitive?

Your AI leaves out names, partner deal terms, revenue figures and anything from private channels, and you can drop or edit any row before you send.

I don’t use AI much for work.

Pick the last option and use the short form. It asks for up to three tasks and takes about 5 minutes.
