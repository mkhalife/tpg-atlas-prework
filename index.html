TPG Atlas pre-work

TPG Atlas is an AI brain for how TPG works, built by the product team. This pre-work helps build and test it: your AI looks at tasks you’ve done recently, asks you about a few more, and writes down the questions about TPG each one needed answered. Those questions become the test Atlas is graded on.

About 10 minutes. You review everything and click Send. It’s anonymous.
