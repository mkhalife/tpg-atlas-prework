# TPG Atlas pre-work

The prompt and button page for the TPG Atlas pre-work. Open the page, click the button for the AI app you use, and press Enter. Your AI reads your own recent sessions on your computer, drafts a few task records from your work with you, and sends only the ones you approve.

- Page: https://mkhalife.github.io/tpg-atlas-prework/
- Prompt: [PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md)

Submissions go to an internal Red Ventures endpoint that requires a Red Ventures Google sign-in and does not record who sent them.
